Stupefy privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Stupefy is a download manager for Windows made by Developer Inayat. It is designed to work without collecting anything about you.

What Stupefy stores

Your download list, history, settings and saved site logins are kept only on your PC, in %LocalAppData%\Stupefy. Passwords and license keys there are encrypted with Windows Data Protection for your Windows account. Nothing is uploaded, synced or shared.

No telemetry, no analytics, no ads

Stupefy has no usage tracking of any kind and does not contact the developer.

Network connections Stupefy makes on its own

  • The sites you download from.
  • This website, about once a day, to read one small file that says which version is the newest (/updates/latest.json). The request carries no information about you or your downloads beyond what any web request carries (your IP address and the browser-style identification string). If you install an update, the installer is downloaded from this website too. The daily check can be switched off in Settings → General.
  • github.com, to download and about once a week update the open-source video tools it relies on (yt-dlp and FFmpeg).
  • api.gumroad.com, only if you activate a Stupefy Pro license: the license key you typed is sent to check it, and re-checked in the background now and then. Like any web request this shows Gumroad your IP address; Gumroad's own privacy policy covers your purchase. The e-mail address Gumroad reports back is stored on your PC in encrypted form only.

Browser extension (Stupefy Companion)

It talks only to the Stupefy app on your own computer (127.0.0.1). When you send a download or a video from a site, it passes that site's cookies to the app on your PC so the download can use your login; they never leave your computer. You can switch cookie sending off in the extension's options. The extension collects no browsing data.

This website

It is static pages with no analytics, cookies or forms. It is published with GitHub Pages, whose host keeps ordinary server logs, as every host does.

Children

Stupefy is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Contact

The e-mail address in the app's About dialog, or via developerinayat.com.
